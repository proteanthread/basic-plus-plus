pacman Tile Maze & Ghost AI State Machine Arcade Template
======================================================================

The pacman template implements grid maze pathfinding, wraparound tunnels,
and 4-state ghost AI personalities (Blinky, Pinky, Inky, Clyde).


FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Tile corner turning queues and wrap tunnels.
  - Ghost AI states (Chase, Scatter, Frightened, Eaten).
  - Energizer timer and ghost eating score multipliers.
