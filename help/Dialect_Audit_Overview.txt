
Dialect Audit Overview
======================

This document lists all keywords in BASIC++ that are constrained to specific historical dialects. Keywords not listed here are universal (DFLAG_ALL) and available under all settings.


Keywords by Dialect Flag Constraints
------------------------------------


DFLAG_DECB
~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: IMAGE, RECORD, UNLESS


DFLAG_E116
~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: ASK, CAUSE, CONTINUE, DET, POINTER, RETRY, REWRITE, SET, USE, WHEN


DFLAG_E116 | DFLAG_SBAS | DFLAG_DECB
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: MAT


DFLAG_GWBS
~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: ERDEV, EXTERR


DFLAG_GWBS | DFLAG_QBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: IOCTL


DFLAG_GWQB
~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: AUTO, BEEP, CDBL, CHDIR, CINT, CIRCLE, CLEAR, COLOR, COM, COMMON, CSNG, CSRLIN, CVD, CVI, CVS, DATE, DRAW, EDIT, ENVIRON, EQV, FIELD, FILES, FIX, FRE, GET, HEX, IMP, INKEY, INP, KEY, KILL, LLIST, LOC, LOCATE, LOCK, LOF, LPOS, LPRINT, LSET, MKD, MKDIR, MKI, MKS, NAME, OCT, OUT, PAINT, PALETTE, PCOPY, PEN, PLAY, PMAP, POINT, POS, PRESET, PSET, PUT, RESET, RMDIR, RSET, SCREEN, SEG, SHELL, SOUND, SPACE, SPC, STICK, STRIG, STRING, TIME, TIMER, TROFF, TRON, UNLOCK, USR, VARPTR, VIEW, WAIT, WIDTH, WINDOW, WRITE


DFLAG_GWQB | DFLAG_E116
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: AS, CALL, CLOSE, EOF, ERASE, ERL, ERR, ERR$, ERROR, LBOUND, LIKE, LINE, MOD, OPEN, RESUME, UBOUND, XOR


DFLAG_GWQB | DFLAG_E116 | DFLAG_SBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: INSTR, SWAP, USING


DFLAG_GWQB | DFLAG_EC55 | DFLAG_E116 | DFLAG_SBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: RANDOMIZE


DFLAG_GWQB | DFLAG_MBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: CHAIN, MERGE


DFLAG_MSALL | DFLAG_E116
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: CLS


DFLAG_MSALL | DFLAG_EC55 | DFLAG_E116 | DFLAG_SBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: DATA, DIM, LEN, READ, RESTORE, SGN


DFLAG_MSBASIC
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: DEFDBL, DEFINT, DEFSNG, DEFSTR, PEEK, POKE


DFLAG_MSBASIC | DFLAG_E116 | DFLAG_SBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: ELSE, LEFT, MID, RIGHT


DFLAG_MSBASIC | DFLAG_EC55 | DFLAG_E116 | DFLAG_SBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: ASC, ATN, CHR, COS, DEF, EXP, FN, LOG, SIN, SQR, STR, TAN, VAL


DFLAG_QBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: REDIM, SEEK, SHARED, SLEEP, STATIC, TYPE


DFLAG_QBAS | DFLAG_E116
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: ACCESS, CASE, CONST, DECLARE, DO, ELSEIF, ENDIF, EXIT, FUNCTION, IS, LCASE, LOOP, LTRIM, RTRIM, SELECT, SUB, UCASE, UNTIL


DFLAG_SBAS
~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: BY, UNLESS


DFLAG_STRUCT
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Keywords: WEND, WHILE
