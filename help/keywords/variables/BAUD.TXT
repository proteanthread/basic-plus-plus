------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DESCRIPTION & USAGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Configures simulated transmission speed in bps (from vintage Baudot 45.45 bps
up to 115200+ bps) on channel (0 for console).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYNTAX
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    BAUD rate# | BAUD = rate# | BAUD [#]channel%, rate#
