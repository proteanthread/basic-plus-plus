LOOP Structured Loop Termination Statement
==========================================

Marks the end of a DO structured loop block, optionally testing a continuation or exit condition.


SYNTAX
----------------------------------------------------------------------

    LOOP [{WHILE | UNTIL} condition]


OPERATIONAL NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Pairs with DO statement.


CODE EXAMPLES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

    10 DO
    20   X = X + 1
    30 LOOP UNTIL X >= 10
