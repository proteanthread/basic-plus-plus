TROFF Turn Off Line Tracing Statement
=====================================

Disables line number execution tracing (GW-BASIC compatibility command).


SYNTAX
----------------------------------------------------------------------

    TROFF


OPERATIONAL NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Equivalent to TRACE OFF.


CODE EXAMPLES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

    10 TRON : REM Enable tracing
    20 X = 10
    30 TROFF : REM Disable tracing
