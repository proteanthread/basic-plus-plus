------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DESCRIPTION & USAGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Streams data continuously from source file/device channel to destination
channel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYNTAX
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    PIPE #src_ch TO #dst_ch [BUFFER size] | STREAMPIPE #src, #dst
