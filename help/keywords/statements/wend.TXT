------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DESCRIPTION & USAGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Marks the end of a WHILE loop block and jumps back to re-evaluate the WHILE
condition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYNTAX
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
