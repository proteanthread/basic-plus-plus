break Execution Breakpoint Statement
====================================

Configures or triggers an immediate breakpoint in the program execution engine.


SYNTAX
----------------------------------------------------------------------

    BREAK [ON | OFF]


OPERATIONAL NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Integrates with interactive debugger and DAP server.


CODE EXAMPLES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

    10 BREAK ON
    20 FOR I = 1 TO 10
    30   IF I = 5 THEN BREAK
    40 NEXT I
