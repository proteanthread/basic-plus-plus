------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DESCRIPTION & USAGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transmits connectionless low-latency 2.4 GHz packets between ESP32 peers
without Wi-Fi router.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYNTAX
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ESPNOW.INIT [channel] | ESPNOW.ADD.PEER mac$ [, channel] | ESPNOW.SEND mac$, data$ | ESPNOW.RECV var$
