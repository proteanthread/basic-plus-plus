1. DESCRIPTION & USAGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Returns the trigonometric sine of an angle (radians by default, degrees or
grads if DEGREE/GRAD mode).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYNTAX
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
