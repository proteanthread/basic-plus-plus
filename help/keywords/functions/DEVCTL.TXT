------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DESCRIPTION & USAGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Directly executes a low-level IOCTL or control command on a device without
requiring OPEN.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYNTAX
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
