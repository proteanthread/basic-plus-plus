mod_arrayext Extended Array Operations Module (libflex)
======================================================================

The mod_arrayext module implements high-performance sorting, searching,
reversing, and slicing for BASIC arrays.


OPERATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  - ARRAY.SORT array() [, ASC | DESC]
  - idx% = ARRAY.SEARCH(array(), target)
  - ARRAY.REVERSE array()
  - ARRAY.COPY src(), dst()
