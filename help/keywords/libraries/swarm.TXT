swarm Boids Flocking & Particle Swarm Simulation
======================================================================

The swarm library implements the Boids flocking algorithm (Separation,
Alignment, Cohesion) and particle swarm physics.


RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Separation: Avoid colliding with nearby flockmates.
  2. Alignment : Steer in the same direction as neighbors.
  3. Cohesion  : Stay close to the center of the local flock.
