custom_statement Custom Statement Extension Template
======================================================================

Guide and template for implementing custom BASIC++ statement handlers in C.


SAFETY RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Always advance lexer tokens (lex_next).
  - Release evaluated strings via str_release(str_ctx, val.as.string).
