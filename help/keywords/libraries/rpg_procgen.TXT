rpg_procgen Procedural Dungeon Generation & Roguelike Engine
======================================================================

The rpg_procgen library provides BSP dungeon generation, cellular
automata caves, and field-of-view (FOV) shadowcasting.


FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  - BSP tree dungeon room and hallway generator.
  - Cellular automata cave generator.
  - Shadowcasting field-of-view and lighting.
