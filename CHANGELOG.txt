1.5.0  (2026-06-11)
--------------------
  ADDED:
  - SUPER BASIC (Tymshare) dialect: DIALECT "SBAS"
    16th built-in dialect. Developed by Mark Alexander and Dan
    Streng for the SDS 940 mainframe, circa 1968. One of the
    most advanced BASIC dialects of the 1960s.
  - Extended math functions from SUPER BASIC:
    ASIN(x), ACOS(x), SINH(x), COSH(x), TANH(x),
    LOG10(x), LOG2(x), COMP(a,b), PDIF(a,b), PI
  - UNLESS statement (JOSS-style negated conditional):
    UNLESS condition THEN statement
    Supports both single-line and block forms.
  - BY keyword as alternative to STEP in FOR loops:
    FOR I = 1 TO 10 BY 2
  - SCRATCH command (alias for KILL, file deletion)
  - UNSAVE command (delete current program file)
